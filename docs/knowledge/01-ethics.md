## 1. Ethics (non-negotiable, highest priority — applies to every contract and every UI)

No riba (interest), no gambling/chance-based payout, no speculation, no predatory tokenomics, no hidden/manipulative incentives, no admin abuse.

**This extends beyond intended use.** Don't build any mechanism, feature, or primitive that a third party could repurpose or misuse to enable haram activity, even if the intended use is legitimate — e.g. never build a generic staking/wagering primitive that could be reused as a betting pool.

**Not banned:** a stake/slash/settlement mechanism where the outcome is a deterministic consequence of a judged, evidence-based verdict (e.g. Ledger of Record's arbitration deposits, Copyleft's dispute settlement). That's a security-deposit pattern, not gambling — there's no odds or chance element, only consequence tied to evidence, and it isn't generically reusable for gambling.

Default project type is still a neutral utility (registry, log, workflow tracker, activity system) unless the concept specifically calls for settlement mechanics.
